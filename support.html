Common questions

How do I reach the Parent area?

Tap the Parent button on the home screen and solve the short maths question. This keeps parent settings out of a young child's reach.

Does my child need an account or the internet?

No account is needed. The app works offline once lessons are downloaded, and it never asks your child to sign in.

Is my child's data private?

Yes. The app collects nothing and sends nothing off the device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Some optional activities let your child say a word aloud and get a friendly star rating. The audio is scored on the device and is never saved or sent anywhere. If you decline, your child gets a listening or matching activity instead.

How do I set a daily time limit?

Open the Parent area and set a daily limit. When the time is up, the app shows a gentle break screen.
